Roadmap to the Platinum

Built from the official trophy descriptions. Estimated difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough, no mission replay — but free roam after the story, so nothing is missable except possibly one meditation (see Missables). Keep cheats off your trophy save: cheats disable trophies and saving.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy in the whole set can possibly be missed. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

Save advice

There is no mission replay and no New Game+. The game gives you 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter, then rotate the rest during free roam — that is the safety net PowerPyx recommends. Keep cheats off the trophy save entirely: they disable trophies and saving, and whether reloading restores trophies is disputed. If you want to experiment with cheats, do it on a separate save.

Save & settings

Your progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) the moment you tick a trophy. Export it to a file to back it up or move it to another browser, and import that file back here.

Export file format: a JSON list of earned trophy names, e.g. ["Vice Got a Price!", "Just Like Paradise"].

Spoiler setting

When “Hide story spoilers until earned” is on, the 28 hidden trophies show only as “Hidden trophy” — no name, no description, no guide — until you tick them earned. The setting is saved with your progress.
