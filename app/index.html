Roadmap to the Platinum

Built from the official trophy descriptions. Estimated difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough, no mission replay. Free roam opens after the story. Only one trophy is potentially missable (see Missables), and one has no established method yet (Bad Luck, I Guess). Keep cheats off your trophy save.

Missable trophies

One trophy is potentially missable. One more has no established method yet, so whether it can be missed is not established either. The other 53 have no known missable step. Story milestones are earned during required story progression.

Save advice

There is no mission replay and no New Game+. The game gives you 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter, then rotate the rest during free roam — that is the safety net PowerPyx recommends. Keep cheats off the trophy save entirely: they disable trophies and saving. If you want to experiment with cheats, do it on a separate save.

Save & settings

Your progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) the moment you tick a trophy, type a note, or change a counter. Export it to back it up or move it to another browser, and import it back here. Export and import carry your earned ticks, your per-trophy notes, your counters, your next-up focus, and the spoiler setting.

Export format: earned trophy names, notes, counters, focus and the spoiler setting. Files exported by the first version of this guide (a plain list of names) still import.

Spoiler setting

When “Hide story spoilers until earned” is on, the 28 hidden trophies show only as “Hidden trophy” until you tick them earned. The setting is saved with your progress.

About this guide

Unofficial fan guide. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games or PlayStation. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown as PlayStation publishes them. Guides are written in this entry’s own words; methods played firsthand are credited to PowerPyx’s GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (Nov 19–20, 2026), with GTABase where noted. Anything not yet confirmed in the game is labelled “not yet checked in the game” — never presented as fact. No Rockstar logo or official artwork appears in this guide.

Counters show the trophy’s own target, with the larger 100%-completion target shown separately where it differs. Each counter names its source — counters are community-reported, not platform data. Strangers, Money and Combat are editorial content tags, not official PlayStation categories: Money here includes robberies, and the Strangers tag lists character questlines, only four of which count toward the 8-strand Strangers & Freaks trophy. The Next-up planner is built from your earned ticks, so importing them restores it.
